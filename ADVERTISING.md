## A/B Testing Strategy

**Week 1:** Test 3 ad variations
- Campaign 1 (Pain Point)
- Campaign 2 (Value Prop)  
- Campaign 4 (Urgency)

**Run each with:**
- Same budget (KES 700/day each)
- Same audience
- Different copy

**After 3 days:**
- Keep winner
- Kill losers
- Scale winning ad budget

---

## Success Metrics

**Target KPIs:**
- Click-through rate (CTR): >2%
- Cost per click (CPC): <KES 5
- Cost per signup: <KES 20
- Conversion rate: >10%

**Decision Criteria:**
After spending KES 5,000:
- ✅ If 250+ signups → Keep running
- ⚠️ If 100-250 signups → Adjust copy/targeting
- ❌ If <100 signups → Pause and rethink

## Pro Tips

1. **Post at Peak Times:**
   - 7-9am (before class)
   - 12-2pm (lunch break)
   - 8-11pm (study time)

2. **Use Kenyan English:**
   - "Comrade" not "student"
   - "Units" not "courses"
   - "CATs" not "quizzes"

3. **Urgency Works:**
   - "First 500 only"
   - "Limited time"
   - "Launching this week"

4. **Mobile-First:**
   - 90% will see on phone
   - Keep text short
   - Big, clear CTA buttons

5. **Test Kenyan vs English:**
   - Try Sheng/Swahili phrases
   - "Msee" "Comrades" "Fiti sana"
   - See what resonates
